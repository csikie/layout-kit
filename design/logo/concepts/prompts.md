# Raster exploration prompts

## 01-open-frame

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: raster exploration for a future hand-authored vector logo for Layout Kit, typed React layout primitives. Primary request: An open page frame: a heavy near-black olive angular L-shaped structural block forming left side and bottom, with a lime horizontal header block nested in its open upper right. Two solid interlocking orthogonal pieces; intentional large open white counterspace. Abstract construction, not a literal letter, not a dashboard. Explore a memorable stepped silhouette. Composition: one large centered mark on plain white, no labels, no text, no mockup. Style: strictly flat crisp geometric shapes, square integer-grid logic, calm, minimal, two colors only: vivid yellow-green lime and almost black olive. Design must survive 16px favicon. No rounded-square container, gradients, blur, shadows, thin lines, perspective, texture, letters, or existing brand references. This is one distinct concept, not a sheet of alternatives.
